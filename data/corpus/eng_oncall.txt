Aether Dynamics Engineering — On-Call Handbook
Document ID: ENG-OC-009
Team: Platform Reliability

Primary on-call engineers rotate weekly, starting Monday 09:00 UTC.
Secondary on-call provides backup and must acknowledge escalations within 15 minutes.
Severity-1 incidents require a status update in the #incidents channel every 30 minutes until mitigation.
Severity-2 incidents require updates every 2 hours during business hours.
On-call compensation is 150 USD per primary week and 50 USD per secondary week, paid monthly.
Engineers on primary on-call must remain within network connectivity sufficient for SSH and paging response within 10 minutes.
Post-incident reviews (PIRs) are mandatory for all Severity-1 events and must be published within 5 business days.
Handoff notes must be written in the on-call runbook wiki before the rotation ends.
