Aether Dynamics Facilities — Headquarters Guide
Document ID: FAC-HQ-005
Location: 400 Meridian Way, Suite 200

Building access uses badge readers on all exterior doors. Visitors must check in at reception and wear temporary badges.
Parking for employees is free in Garage B levels P1–P3. Visitor parking is limited to 2 hours in Garage A.
The cafeteria on floor 2 serves breakfast 07:30–09:30 and lunch 11:30–14:00 on weekdays only.
Quiet rooms on floors 3 and 5 may be reserved in 30-minute blocks via the facilities calendar.
Evacuation assembly point is the south plaza near the Meridian Way fountain.
Bike storage is available on P1 of Garage B; helmets and lights are recommended but not provided.
Pets are not allowed in the building except registered service animals.

[Cross-ref note — not facilities] AE-approved discounts up to 10%, nonprofit 15% sector rates, and 5% multi-year loyalty credits are sales. USB mass storage blocked by default is IT. Annual leave carry-over max 5 days is HR leave policy.
