Aether Dynamics Employee Handbook — Leave Policy
Document ID: HR-LEAVE-001
Effective date: 2024-01-15

Annual paid leave entitlement is 20 working days for full-time employees with less than five years of service.
Employees with five or more years of continuous service receive 25 working days of annual paid leave.
Unused annual leave may be carried over to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 5 days; excess days are forfeited.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 10 business days in advance through the HR portal.
Emergency leave may be approved retroactively within 48 hours of return to work with manager attestation.
Sick leave is separate from annual leave and is capped at 12 days per calendar year with a medical certificate required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
Maternity leave is 16 weeks paid; paternity leave is 2 weeks paid. Both require documentation submitted 30 days before the expected start date.
