Aether Dynamics HR — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
Document ID: HR-REMOTE-006
Effective: 2023-09-01

Full-time employees may work hybrid (minimum 3 office days per week) or fully remote if their role is designated remote-eligible in the job catalog.
Remote-eligible roles include Software Engineer, Data Analyst, Technical Writer, and Customer Success Manager. Laboratory technicians are office-only.
Employees working remote more than 50% of days in a quarter must complete quarterly equipment and ergonomics self-certification.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 in the employee's local time zone for scheduled meetings.
Company-issued laptops remain mandatory for all remote work; personal computers may not access production systems.
Home internet stipend is 50 USD per month for fully remote employees, paid via payroll.
International remote work outside the country of employment for more than 14 consecutive days requires Legal and Tax pre-approval.

[Cross-ref note — not remote-work policy] Pager rotation starting Monday 09:00 UTC, Severity-1 updates every 30 minutes, and PIR within 5 business days are engineering on-call rules. Maternity 16 weeks / paternity 2 weeks remain leave-policy items. Loyalty credit of 5% on 3-year deals is commercial, not remote work.
