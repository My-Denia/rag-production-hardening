Aether Dynamics Product Brief — Project Nova
Document ID: PRD-NOVA-03
Status: Generally Available since 2024-02-20

Project Nova is the company's real-time analytics dashboard for manufacturing line telemetry.
Nova supports ingestion from MQTT brokers and OPC-UA endpoints at up to 50,000 events per second per cluster.
Default data retention in Nova Standard tier is 30 days; Nova Enterprise extends retention to 365 days.
Nova export formats include CSV, Parquet, and Prometheus remote-write.
The free trial of Nova lasts 14 days and is limited to 2 data sources and 1 million events total.
Customer success contacts for Nova are listed at nova-support@aetherdynamics.example.
Nova does not process personal health information (PHI); healthcare customers must use the separate Helix product line.
SLA for Nova Enterprise is 99.9% monthly uptime; Standard tier has no formal SLA.
