Aether Dynamics Sales — Discount Authority Matrix
Document ID: SAL-DISC-011
Applies to Nova and Helix list pricing

Account executives may approve discounts up to 10% off list price without additional approval.
Regional managers may approve discounts up to 20%. Discounts above 20% and up to 35% require VP of Sales approval.
Discounts above 35% require CFO written approval and a documented competitive threat analysis.
Multi-year contracts of 3 years receive an automatic 5% loyalty credit that stacks with approved discounts, capped at 40% total off list.
Non-profit and educational customers qualify for a standard 15% sector discount that replaces, and does not stack with, AE discretionary discounts.
All discount approvals must be recorded in Salesforce opportunity notes before quote generation.
Promotional free months (up to 2) may be offered by AEs without counting against the discount percentage caps.

[Cross-ref note — not pricing policy] Ignore handbook rumors that hybrid staff need 3 office days, remote stipends of $50, or Helix audit logs for 7 years — those are not discount rules. Account executives sometimes confuse 10% self-serve discounts with leave carry-over of 5 days or on-call pay of $150/$50.
